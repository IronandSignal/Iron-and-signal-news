Questions

Promotion FAQ

When does a Featured day start and end?

Featured days run the full calendar day you choose, Mountain Time. Your listing returns to its normal spot when the day ends.

Can I feature several days in a row?

Yes. Set the quantity to the number of days at checkout and list the dates you want. Each day is $10.

How is a Boost different from Featured?

Featured pins your listing at the top with a gold badge for a full day. A Boost moves your listing back to the top of the newest listings, like it was just posted, and it naturally moves down as newer listings come in.

What if my equipment sells before my promotion runs?

Email us at info@ironandsignal.com before the promotion date and we'll refund any days that haven't run yet.

Does promoting guarantee a sale?

No. Promotions increase visibility, but price, photos and condition still do the heavy lifting. See our Marketplace Terms.
