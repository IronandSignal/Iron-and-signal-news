Marketplace Terms

The rules for buying, selling and listing on the Iron & Signal Marketplace.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

These Marketplace Terms apply to everyone who posts, browses or responds to a listing on the Iron & Signal Marketplace at ironandsignal.com. By submitting a listing or contacting a seller through the site, you agree to these terms.

The short version: Iron & Signal is a listing platform. We are not the buyer or seller, we don't hold money or equipment, and every deal is between the buyer and the seller. Inspect before you pay.

1. What Iron & Signal does

We publish listings for construction, survey and mining equipment and technology submitted by sellers. We review listings before they go live, but we do not inspect, verify, own, store, ship or guarantee any equipment, and we are not a party to any sale.

2. Who can use the Marketplace

  • You must be at least 18 and able to enter a binding contract.
  • The Marketplace is not available to buyers or sellers located in China or Russia, and equipment located in or headed to China or Russia may not be listed or sold through the site.
  • You may not use the Marketplace if you, your business or the equipment's destination is subject to U.S. sanctions or trade restrictions.

3. Seller responsibilities

When you submit a listing, you confirm that:

  • You own the equipment or are authorized to sell it.
  • Your description, photos, year, hours, condition and price are accurate and not misleading.
  • The equipment is free of undisclosed liens, or you disclose them in the listing.
  • You will follow all laws that apply to your sale, including export control, sanctions, tax, titling and registration rules.
  • You will update or remove your listing promptly once the equipment is sold or no longer available.

Contact details you choose to show on a listing will be visible to the public.

4. Buyer responsibilities

  • Inspect equipment in person, or have a qualified inspector do it, before paying.
  • Verify serial numbers and PINs, and check for liens and theft records.
  • Use secure payment methods. Never wire money to someone you haven't verified.
  • Some survey, GNSS, LiDAR and drone equipment can be subject to export controls. If you plan to move equipment across borders, make sure you have the right approvals.

5. Prohibited listings

You may not list:

  • Stolen equipment, or equipment you don't have the right to sell.
  • Weapons, military or defense items, or anything designed for military use.
  • Items located in, or being sold to anyone in, China or Russia, or to any sanctioned person or country.
  • Counterfeit, recalled or unsafe equipment presented as safe for use.
  • Equipment with altered, removed or fake serial numbers.
  • Services, real estate, or anything unrelated to construction, survey or mining equipment and technology.
  • Listings that are spam, duplicates, bait-and-switch offers or scams.

6. Our review and removal rights

We review every listing before it goes live and may edit formatting, request changes, decline, or remove any listing at any time, for any reason, including suspected fraud or a violation of these terms. Approval of a listing is not an endorsement of the seller or the equipment.

7. Featured listings and fees

Basic listings are free. Featured listings, which receive a badge and higher placement, are an optional paid upgrade. Pricing and duration are confirmed with the seller before any charge. Featured fees pay for placement only and do not guarantee a sale.

8. No warranties

The Marketplace and all listings are provided "as is." Iron & Signal makes no warranties about any equipment, seller, buyer, price or listing, including warranties of condition, fitness for a particular purpose or title.

9. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent allowed by law, Iron & Signal is not liable for any loss or damage arising from a transaction between users, from a listing, or from use of the Marketplace. Disputes about a sale are between the buyer and the seller.

10. Reporting a problem

If you see a suspicious listing or have a problem with a buyer or seller, email info@ironandsignal.com with the listing link and details. We'll review it and take action where appropriate.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top shows when they last changed. Continued use of the Marketplace means you accept the updated terms.
