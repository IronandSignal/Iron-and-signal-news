Buyer safety

Buy smart.

Inspect before you pay. See the machine in person or send a trusted mechanic or inspector.
Verify the serial number. Match the PIN or serial to the paperwork and run it for liens and theft records.
Never wire money to a stranger. For big deals, use a reputable equipment escrow service or pay at pickup.
If the price looks too good, it probably is. Watch for pushy sellers, overseas shipping requests and refusals to meet.
